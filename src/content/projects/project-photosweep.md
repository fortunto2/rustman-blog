# PhotoSweep

[PhotoSweep](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6794369239) frees the space on an
iPhone that you actually control: the large videos and screenshots sitting in the photo
library. A dashboard shows what the library is made of, videos are listed by size with
thumbnails, and screenshots clear in a few taps.

## The interesting decision is what it refuses to claim

iOS sandboxes every app. No app can read or clear another app's cache, and none can touch
"System Data" — the category that swallows the most space and worries people the most. Apps
in this category advertise otherwise; what they clear is their own cache.

PhotoSweep says so on the store page and inside the app, then does the next best thing:
deep links and plain steps for clearing Telegram, Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok and YouTube
by hand.

Being the app that admits the limit is a worse pitch and a better product. In a category
built on a claim the platform forbids, the honest version is the differentiated one.

Free, no ads.
