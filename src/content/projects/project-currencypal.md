# CurrencyPals

Every converter asks the same two questions first: from what, and to what. Then you get the
answer, realise you wanted it the other way, and swap.

[CurrencyPals](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6759005730) removes the question.
Thirty-six rows, all of them editable — type an amount anywhere and every other row updates
at once. There is no direction to choose because there is no direction.

Thirty-one world currencies plus Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP and BNB. A 30-day chart for
any pair with its high, low and change. Reorder the list to match how you travel.

## It works where you need it

Rates are cached on the device the first time they load, so it converts on a plane, at a
border crossing, or on a foreign SIM that has not connected yet — the three places a
traveller actually needs a converter and the three places a network request fails. A badge
at the top always says how old the numbers are, because a stale rate presented as current
is worse than no rate.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Free.
