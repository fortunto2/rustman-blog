# Live2play

[Live2play](https://superduperai.co/products/live2play/) is an iPhone camera for musicians who
film themselves playing. The app opens straight into a live vertical camera with one big record
button, and clips land in their own Photos album. It is free on the
[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794370589), with no account and no in-app purchases.

What it does:

- **One-tap reel.** Camera is live on launch; tap to focus and meter, lock exposure, nudge EV.
  Start from a hardware button or a Siri shortcut, with a Live Activity while recording.
- **The sound you choose.** Built-in mic, a USB-C interface, Bluetooth, or a clean master mix sent
  from your computer over WiFi by the companion Mac app, instead of room noise.
- **Movement as an instrument.** Apple's Vision tracks body, hands and face on the phone; that
  drives an overlay, a built-in synth, or gesture MIDI sent to the computer.
- **Loops, collage, editing.** Layer takes into a synced loop grid, arrange them as a collage, trim
  and merge clips on the phone.
- **Go live** to YouTube or any RTMP/RTMPS server.

It started as `reelcam`, a companion camera for livehub (a live-performance rig): a vertical reel
from the side of the stage with the master mix as its soundtrack. The App Store name is Live2play,
subtitle "Reel camera for musicians".

Its page, privacy policy and support live on superduperai.co, the company site; the old
`live2play.superduperai.co` subdomain redirects there.
