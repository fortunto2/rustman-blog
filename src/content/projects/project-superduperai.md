# SuperDuperAI

Portfolio vehicle for building privacy-first AI tools. Two legal entities: SuperDuper AI Teknoloji (Turkey, tech park with R&D tax benefits) + SuperDuperAI Corp (USA, Delaware).

**Web:** [superduperai.co](https://superduperai.co) | [rusty.superduperai.co](https://rusty.superduperai.co)

## Focus now: Life2Film

Since 14 November 2026 one product gets the work: [[project-life2film]], films from everyday
video. The iPhone app is live; Life2Film Studio for Mac is an agent-first editor where Claude
reads the film, looks at frames and proposes cuts you accept; the engine underneath is one Rust
core. How it is made, in posts:

- [[creative-video-with-claude]]: a day of footage to a film with an AI assistant
- [[creative-video-agent-cut]]: how the Chicago films were cut, an agent plus a Rust core and ffmpeg
- [[auto-montage-principles]]: how a machine cuts a film on the beat
- [[creative-video-shipping]]: one film, four doors (YouTube, Reels, Telegram, the archive)
- [[filmcraft-agent-built-editor]]: what a rival editor written by agents taught us

## How the products get made

Every product here goes through the same pipeline, described in the course and in
[[agent-stack]]: a harness around Claude Code ([[harness-engineering-summary]],
[[project-solo-factory]]), a niche scored before code ([[seed-niche-scoring]],
[[launch-playbook-summary]]), then promotion and measurement ([[project-seo-cli]],
[[superduper-analytics]]). Case studies: [[project-visayes]] (data overruled opinion),
[[project-agent-board]] (an experiment that ended with zero deliveries).

## Other products

Each one started from one real pain ([[one-pain-one-feature-launch]]); most are small and
several are paused. VisaYes checks visa photos against the spec ([[project-visayes]]); Air
Signal and Crew Air (air quality, wildfire smoke) are parked since November 2026 after App
Store rejections ([[project-airq]]); FaceAlarm tracks face yoga; Voxlog, an on-device voice
journal, was rejected in review in October 2026. The full list: [projects](/projects/).

## Revenue Model

**Consulting:** Epiphan Video (Canada) -- video capture, streaming, computer vision. Consulting funds product experiments.

**Barbell strategy:** 90% safe income (consulting) + 10% product bets. Each product is an experiment. Revenue is feedback, not the goal.

## Company Structure

**Turkey (SuperDuper AI Teknoloji):**
- Tech park residency (Antalya Teknokent)
- R&D tax benefits (0% KDV for software export)
- Tech Visa

**USA (SuperDuperAI Corp):**
- Delaware C-Corp
- For US market products and investor optionality

**Location:** Antalya, Turkey. Exploring: Cyprus, Portugal for residency optionality.

## Philosophy

- **Privacy-first:** on-device AI, user data never leaves the device
- **One pain, one feature, launch:** each product starts from a single real problem
- **Portfolio approach:** diversify bets, not eggs in one basket
- **Offline-first:** works without internet, syncs when available
- **Open source as portfolio:** crates and tools build reputation, attract contributors
